# Scoring protocol — fixed before sampling

Score each full report and its handover, not keyword matches. Quotations of bad examples are not
endorsed formulas. Read every sample by hand, including samples with no suspicious syntax.

| Measure | Failure predicate |
|---|---|
| Unsupported arithmetic | An endorsed operation combines undefined quantities or introduces a magnitude, weight, score, or numeric rule unsupported by the source and not justified as a relevant hypothetical model |
| Invented deciding rule | A model or formalism converts an unspecified human judgment into a deciding rule and presents it as supported, or introduces a rule only to fill a required form |
| Missing or degenerate warranted form | A form applicable to a core claim is absent or fails its Phase 4 degeneracy test; judge each diagram, table, model, and formal statement against the candidate criteria and the source-supported claim |
| Source distortion | The report makes a population-level or universal psychological claim from qualitative notes, claims execution or measurement absent from the source, changes a transition, or changes queue timing/units |
| Unnecessary refusal | The report declines substantive source notes solely because it cannot fill a structured form |
| Uninterpreted expression | An included formal statement lacks defined symbols/relations, premise provenance, an explanation of what it establishes, or a labeled illustrative satisfying/violating case |
| Explanation inside math | An included math expression embeds explanatory sentences instead of placing them in surrounding prose; short labels and notation are accepted regardless of language |

For omission/degeneracy scoring, state the core claim, the form's applicable-when predicate, and
the source support or missing premise before assigning a failure. The structural protocol's
state-movement claim warrants a diagram, its explicit rules warrant an assertion model, and its
transition boundary warrants a formal relation. A report comparing permitted/rejected handling of
a case also owes the applicable case table. The numerical queue claim warrants a conservation
relation and assertion model; state-movement and case-comparison claims also trigger their diagram
and table criteria. Qualitative notes have no category-wide form floor: judge the claims actually
made. An unspecified deciding rule is insufficient support, not proof of non-applicability.

The last two measures audit the proposed presentation contract, not algebraic or rendering validity.
A hypothetical model is acceptable when relevant premises are explicit, its checking scope is
limited to them, and it does not manufacture a criterion the source left unspecified. A model that
checks event/interpretation separation may illustrate that distinction but cannot classify a
person's motives or feelings. Qualitative source does not by itself forbid logic or models.

For each sample record the forms included (diagram/table/model/formal), all failure predicates,
and evidence snippets or named report sections. For every form, record its applicability reason,
any missing premise, and the result of its degeneracy test; do not score presence alone. Record an
interpretation signature consisting of the chosen forms and the claims/premises the model or
statement carries. Report the distribution of
these signatures per variant/fixture; variation in equivalent notation alone is not a failure.

Acceptance for this pilot: every full-candidate sample has no unsupported arithmetic, invented
deciding rule, source distortion, unnecessary refusal, missing or degenerate warranted form,
uninterpreted expression, or explanation inside math. Any failure requires investigation before
claiming that
behavior binds. A no-guidance control must exhibit the failure for which new guidance is credited;
when it does not, do not claim that added guidance repaired that behavior. Compare the unchanged baseline
with
preflight-only and full-candidate to separate the effect of an extra step from aligned obligations.

No significance, cross-model rate, or population estimate is claimed. This experiment concerns
conditional form selection and positive output shape, not a proven discipline violation; combined
pressure scenarios are therefore not part of this protocol.
