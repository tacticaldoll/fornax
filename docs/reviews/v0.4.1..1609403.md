Structural Appendix
check_citations.citations / module_symbols / citable — one file's lines come from markdown_links.prose_lines for Markdown and splitlines otherwise, so fenced quotations are excluded; LINE_CITATION matches are dropped when they sit inside a URL_AUTHORITY span, which now covers both the scheme form and the network-path form. SYMBOL_CITATION requires a lowercase module head and one or two dotted parts, so a class member is reachable and a bare backticked name is not — which is what leaves the VERDICT-CONTROL guard unverified (Claim 1). module_symbols collects top-level functions, classes with their method and assignment members, and top-level assignments; three states (None, a mapping, a reason) are already distinguished, which is the shape finding 6 asks evidence_currency.fingerprint to adopt.

evidence_currency.fingerprint / resolved_inside / check — the read is guarded twice, lexically by spelled_inside through host_paths (both host grammars) and by resolution through path_boundary, so a registry-relative symlink at /etc/hosts is refused rather than hashed. That control is correct and is why Gate 8 passes for this module; the defect is entirely in what the caller is told when the guard, the read or the heading lookup gives out.

distribution_manifest.install_refs / validate_install_pins — install_refs returns facts (refs and Unreads) and never prints, matching path_boundary's convention; validate_install_pins mixes the listing, the policy and the diagnostics in one body. Its closure test (opening not in text[match.end():]) asks whether the opening quote character appears anywhere in the remainder of the document rather than within the token's own word or line; in the reviewed documents every path through it still yields the right ref, because a run-on token acquires whitespace and fails RELEASE_REF, so this is recorded here rather than as a finding.
