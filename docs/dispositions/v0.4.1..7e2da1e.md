## Pattern

Seven of the nine findings are in the commit that repaired the previous nine. That is the same shape
the previous record named — a repair's own output is the least-reviewed text in a round — and the
previous record also said the answer is not more care but a gated round. This round is the evidence
for that: it opened Gates 1–8 over the repair commit alone, found seven, and the two it did not find
in code it found in the ledger and the rule.

What is new is cause 7, and it is the sharper reading of the same shape. The guards ledger did not
fail because its author was careless with it; it failed because **it was written in a turn that
could not know what it had to contain.** The findings list is produced by the record, and the record
is written after the repairs. A ledger completed alongside the repairs is completed against a list
that does not exist yet. Moving *when* it is written removes the possibility rather than asking for
more attention — which is the difference between this repair and the two before it.
