Cause 2 holds it. The prior round accepted its causes 1 and 13 against `record_shape`, and repair 1a
built the record-level rules while 13a passed the inner shape. Rules now receive that shape; one
uses it. `AGENTS.md` states the obligation under Script And Dependency Policy: when a module takes
ownership of a behaviour, enumerate every existing implementation of it.

Repairing the two instances will not remove the shape. The pattern-level repair is the sweep
`AGENTS.md` already prescribes, made into something a round performs rather than remembers: after a
repair, enumerate every site the changed unit owns and record each as routed or deliberately
outside. That is a change to how a round is conducted rather than to any unit above, so it is not in
the table and does not route to `plan-implementation` — it goes to `harden-skill`, against
`static-review` and `triage-findings`.

A second and weaker shape: **a property stated where nothing evaluates it.** Cause 3's annotation and
cause 1's docstring claim both drifted from the code under a green gate. The prior round deferred
RULE-ANNOTATED-SHAPE-GETS-DECLARED for precisely this reason — no type checker runs in the gate —
and 3b is that deferral's stated condition arriving.
