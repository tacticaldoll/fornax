## Pattern

Causes 6, 7, 8 and 9 are one shape and it is the sharpest the range has produced: **every one of them
is a defect in the mechanism built to prevent defects, found in the commit that built it.** The
citation rule disagreed with its own checker's subject list, the guard ledger did not hold what its
own sentence promised, the completion clause condemned the commit that wrote it, and the ledger's
own guard was written in a form its own checker cannot read.

The previous record named the same shape and called the answer "reading the repair as a change in
its own right". That is not what caught these — a gated review did, over nineteen modules. The
honest conclusion is narrower than the previous record's: **a repair's author is not the reader who
finds this class, and the pattern-level repair is the gated round, not more care.**

Cause 1 is different and is the only shipped-behaviour defect in the range. It also shows what the
gates buy: six rounds of contract probes over the same tree never asked what happens at a root
without git, because the probes followed the changes and the change was elsewhere.
