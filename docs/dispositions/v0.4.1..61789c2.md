## Pattern

Every finding is in the record-and-ledger machinery; the code passed every gate this round opened.
That is the third round running where the product is clean and the apparatus is not, and it is worth
saying without softening: **the apparatus now costs more findings per round than the thing it
governs.**

Finding 1 is the one that matters, and not for its size. A Disposition Record exists so a later
round can check a settlement. This one named a commit that exists in no clone but mine — I read the
hash while drafting the record, then amended the commit twice to finish a rewrap, and never re-read
it. It would have become unrecoverable rather than merely wrong at the next `git gc`. The repair is
not care; it is that the hash is read after the commit is final, and that every hash a record names
is checked the way a later round would check it. I ran that check over every record in the tree: one
defect, now repaired, and nothing else.

Findings 2 and 5 are the same shape one layer down — a repair that changed the reported symptom and
not the thing the symptom came from. A row was given dashes instead of being moved; a line was split
instead of the paragraph being reflowed. Both records then said the work was done.
