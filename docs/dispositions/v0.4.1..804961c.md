## Pattern

Causes 1, 2 and 3 are one shape and it is the sharpest instance of it yet: **a check written to stop
a class of silent failure contained that class.** The citation checker's whole purpose is to refuse a
citation that cannot be verified, and it silently skipped a misspelled module, silently skipped every
citation form outside one regex spelling, and silently misreported test-module citations. The three
were found by one round, in the commit that added the checker.

Cause 4 is the count rule violated by the paragraph adding a rule beside it, for the third round
running, and one of its instances is in the file where the same ordinal was fixed two rounds ago.

What all four say together: **a repair's own prose and its own new code are the least-reviewed text
in a round, because attention is on the thing being repaired.** The pattern-level answer available
here is not another check — it is that a repair is read as a change in its own right before it is
committed, including its docstrings, its rule text and the negative controls it claims. The three
probes this round's checker repair carries are what that looks like when it is done.

This record is its own instance, twice. Its `Settled by` column first named three commit hashes that
do not exist in this repository, written from the shape of a hash rather than from `git log`. And its
`Verified how` cells first reproduced the two falsifiers verbatim, which the new check refused —
correctly, because a citation in prose is a citation whatever sentence holds it, and a table cell
cannot hold a fence. Those cells now describe what was measured instead of emitting it. Both were
caught before the record was committed, by the reading the paragraph above asks for rather than by any
property of the record; the second was caught by the check itself, on the record announcing it.
