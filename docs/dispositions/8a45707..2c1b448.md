### Pattern

**A cause located inside the unit that carries the finding, when the cause is the unit's remit.**

Causes 1 and 2 hold it. Cause 1 has now been stated three times as a defect in a matcher, and the
matcher is the defect only because the function was asked a question nothing here can answer; each
restatement produced a better proxy, and a proxy for a grammar rule is that rule hand-written under
another name. Cause 2 has been stated twice as a search that needs widening, when the search is the
wrong kind of instrument for what it claims to enumerate.

What made this invisible is structural rather than personal, and it is where the repair belongs. A
review reports on what is there, so a finding takes the shape of a defect in an implementation.
This skill then asks what one change would remove a finding, and a change that removes the
function's remit does not present itself as an answer to that question. `AGENTS.md`'s weighing rule
reaches an **added** check — name what it would have caught and what it costs — and nothing reaches
a **kept** requirement. So no step in the round was pointed at the only question that would have
ended the recurrence: must this unit answer this at all.

The pattern-level repair is that question, asked where a finding recurs. A finding returning after
its recorded Reach changed in full is evidence about the cause, not only about the repair, and the
second such return should force the remit onto the table beside the implementation. That is a
change to this skill and to the weighing rule, not to any unit above, so it is not in the table:
`harden-skill` for the skills, `audit-governance` for whether the weighing rule should reach a kept
requirement as it reaches an added check.

The Security Triage Alert is cause 1 read at its consumer, where a silently shorter word list
becomes a pin that compares clean. It is not a separate shape.
