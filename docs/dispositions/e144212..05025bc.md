### Pattern

**A repair closes the instance the review named and reopens the class one step away.** Three causes
hold it, and all three were produced by the round that was repairing the previous instance of it.

Cause 1 is the third turn of the same screw in one unit. A hash after an operator was missed, and
widening the matcher fixed that and left the guess; removing the guess fixed that and left a new
boundary rule, hand-written, one character to the right. Cause 2 is the same shape in the ledger:
the repair severed the only path by which a guard reached its subject, and the protection that
exists catches a renamed symbol rather than an unreached one. Cause 3 is the same shape in the
registry: the sweep that the repair owed found the surviving caller and registered it, and did not
notice that the repair had moved a grammar decision out of the form the entry's own check searches
for.

The prior round routed this Pattern to `harden-skill` and nothing has changed as a result, which is
itself the finding. The repair is not another instance fix. It is that a round must re-measure what
its own Reach touched — the guards rows naming a unit it changed, the enumerations claiming to see
that unit — before reporting, and the sweep as written does not ask for that. `2c` is that repair
and it is the only one here that reaches the shape.

The Security Triage Alert is not a separate shape. It is cause 1 read at its consumer, where a
silently shorter word list becomes a pin that compares clean.
